I just finished my first data analyst portfolio project: a Customer Churn Analysis for a telecom company. 📊

The question: which customers are leaving, and how much revenue is at risk?

What I found (7,032 customers):
🔹 26.6% of customers churned, about $139K in monthly revenue
🔹 Month-to-month contracts churn at 42.7% vs 2.8% for two-year contracts, and account for 87% of lost revenue
🔹 47.7% of customers leave within their first year
🔹 Fiber optic customers churn at 41.9%, more than double DSL
🔹 Highest-risk group: month-to-month + fiber optic + electronic check, with 60.4% churn

My recommendations: move month-to-month customers to annual plans with incentives, invest in first-year onboarding, and look into fiber optic service quality.
